Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

My Health Journal ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of every user. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and what rights you have over your data. By using the App, you consent to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the App:

  • Account information: your name, primary email address, optional recovery email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, and (optionally) address, blood group, and emergency contact details.
  • Health readings you enter: blood pressure, pulse, and blood glucose values, along with contextual tags (medication timing, meal timing, activity, symptoms, notes) that you choose to record.
  • Device information: a randomly generated device identifier stored locally on your device. This identifier is used only to distinguish devices you sign in from; it is not tied to hardware or location.
  • Usage logs: timestamps of logins, profile changes, exports, backups, and other in-app actions you take. These are kept to help you review your own account history and to alert you of unusual activity.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, request, or store: your precise location, your contacts, your photos, your browsing history, advertising identifiers, or any biometric data. We do not use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and provide secure access to your account.
  • To store and display your readings back to you across your devices.
  • To send transactional emails — verification codes, login alerts, password/PIN reset notifications, and confirmation of account actions.
  • To produce exports (PDF reports, CSV spreadsheets, JSON backups) that you explicitly request.
  • To detect and warn you about potential security issues, such as logins from new devices.

5. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Primary storage: Your readings and profile are stored in a secure cloud database (Firebase Firestore) under your unique user identifier. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Device storage: a local copy of your account data is cached on your device so the App works offline. You can clear this at any time in Settings → Advanced → Account & Data Management → Clear Local Data.
  • Google Drive: If you choose to connect your Google Drive, exports and backups are stored in your own Drive under /My Health Journal/. The Administrator's Drive receives a mirror copy of each export and backup for archival purposes.

6. Email Communications

Your email address is used exclusively for the following:

  • Account registration and login verification.
  • One-Time Passwords (OTPs) for identity confirmation during sensitive operations such as password reset, PIN reset, and email verification.
  • Transactional notifications such as login alerts, backup confirmations, and account security alerts.

We do not send marketing emails, newsletters, or promotional content. You may opt out of the three optional summary emails (daily reminder, weekly summary, monthly summary) at any time in Settings → Advanced → Summary Emails.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in the following narrow circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and database services, Google Drive for optional backups, and a mail delivery service for transactional email. These providers process data strictly on our behalf.
  • Administrator access: As described in the Terms of Service, the Administrator is legally permitted to access, view, or store data and may share it with third parties within the legal scope required by applicable law.
  • Legal obligations: If required by a valid court order, subpoena, or applicable law.

8. Data Retention

Your account and readings are retained for as long as your account remains active. You may delete your account at any time from Settings → Advanced → Account & Data Management → Delete Account. Deletion removes your profile and all readings from our database and deletes your Firebase Auth account.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data at any time by exporting it as PDF, CSV, or JSON from My Health Logs.
  • Correct your profile at any time from the Profile page.
  • Delete your data or your entire account from Settings.
  • Opt out of optional summary emails from Settings → Advanced → Summary Emails.
  • Withdraw Google Drive access at any time by disconnecting Drive in Settings or by revoking the App's permissions from your Google Account.

10. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards: Firebase Authentication for sign-in, HTTPS for all network traffic, OAuth 2.0 for Google Drive access, and per-user access rules in the database. No system is perfectly secure; you are responsible for keeping your password and PIN confidential.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us, and we will delete it.

12. Global Users

Your data may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where Firebase servers are located. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced inside the App. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

About My Health Journal

My Health Journal is a secure, privacy-first application designed to help you log blood pressure, pulse, and blood glucose readings, uncover vital trends, and maintain full control of your personal health data.

Key Features

  • AHA Standard BP Tracking: Record your BP readings and get'em automatically classified (Normal, Elevated, Stage 1, Stage 2, Hypertensive Crisis) following American Heart Association guidelines.
  • Blood Glucose Tracking: Log fasting, pre-meal, post-meal, bedtime, and random glucose readings with per-category clinical classification.
  • Pulse Tracking: Record your resting pulse in beats per minute alongside each blood pressure reading to see how your heart rate trends over time.
  • Measurement Score: Track logging consistency per metric (BP, Pulse, and each glucose category).
  • Contextual Tags: Optionally attach tags (medication, meals, post-activity) to each reading.
  • Visual Analytics: Track health trends across custom date ranges with individual or combined charts.
  • Doctor-Ready Exports: Generate formatted PDF summary reports or CSV data sheets.
  • Medical Records: Store prescriptions and test reports as PDFs in your Drive and the Administrator's Drive.
  • Multi-User Support: Up to 5 distinct accounts per device.
  • App Security: Custom PIN or compatible biometric authentication.
  • Backup Codes: Generate, store, and download a secure backup code for account recovery.
  • Change Logs: Download detailed logs of all account changes.
  • Automated Emails: The app sends emails regularly for actions such as logins, password changes, backups, exports, and account updates to keep you informed. To review every email and its trigger, see the Email Templates and Triggers page.

How to Navigate the App?

My Health Journal is organised around five main screens, reachable from the bottom navigation bar — Home, History, Trends, Settings, and Profile — plus an orange + button in the centre of the bottom navigation for recording a new measurement, and an avatar menu in the top-right corner for account actions.

  1. Home

    Your daily dashboard. It shows three things at once:

    • Current Reading: three equal panels displaying the latest Blood Pressure, Pulse, and Blood Glucose values from today, each with its classification.
    • Today's Snapshot: per-metric summaries — Blood Pressure Today, Pulse Today, and Blood Glucose Today. The Glucose panel is further broken down into its five timing categories (Fasting, Pre Meal, Post Meal, Bedtime, Random), each with its own Average, Highest, Lowest, Count, and Measurement Score.
    • Today's Readings Table: a list of every reading you have logged today, in chronological order, with values and classification pills for each metric.

    Use the blue + Record Measurement button, or the orange + button in the centre of the bottom navigation, to log a new reading at any time.

  2. History

    A complete list of every reading you have ever logged.

    Date filters: Today, 1 Day, 2 Days, 3 Days, 7 Days, 14 Days, 30 Days, 60 Days, 90 Days, 180 Days, or a Custom range (choose any From and To dates).

    Readings are displayed in a table with columns for Serial, Blood Pressure, Pulse, Blood Glucose, and Time. Each value has a small colour-coded classification label below it.

    Actions:

    • View: tap any row to open the full detail of that reading — date, time, window, position, arm, medication, meal, activity, intake, symptoms, and notes.
    • Edit: use the Edit Reading button inside the reading detail view to correct values, unit, category, or timestamp. Every edit is logged with a timestamp under Edit History.
    • Delete: use the Delete Reading button inside the reading detail view.
    • Bulk select: right-click (or long-press on mobile) any reading to reveal checkboxes, then Export or Delete multiple readings at once.

    About editing: readings can be edited after saving. Tap Edit Reading inside the reading detail view to correct values, unit, category, or timestamp. Every edit is logged with a timestamp in the reading's Edit History.

  3. Trends

    Visual analysis of your readings over time.

    Date filters: same options as History — Today through 180 Days, or a Custom range.

    Summary statistics: for the selected range, you see Average, Highest, Lowest, Count, Measurement Score, and Average Classification for Blood Pressure, Pulse, and each glucose category.

    Chart options:

    • Blood Pressure: systolic and diastolic lines over time.
    • Pulse: pulse rate line over time.
    • Blood Glucose: choose a single category (Fasting, Pre Meal, Post Meal, Bedtime, Random) or view all categories together. Each category has its own colour and its own target band shown as a shaded strip behind the line.
    • Combined: stacked panels for Blood Pressure, Pulse, and Blood Glucose all aligned on the same time axis, so you can see how the three metrics move together.

    Below the charts is a table of every reading in the selected range, with columns for Serial, Blood Pressure, Pulse, Blood Glucose, Time, and Average Classification.

  4. Settings

    Configuration for backup, exports, emails, security, and account actions. Opens from either the Settings button in the bottom navigation, or the avatar menu. It has five sections:

    1. Account Management — View Profile, Security Settings (PIN, password, biometric, backup codes), and Privacy & Data (clear local data, clear journal, delete account).
    2. Backup & Exports — Connect Your Google Drive, Automatic Google Drive Backup, Backup to Google Drive, Download Backup File, Export PDF Report, Export CSV Spreadsheet, and Import Backup File.
    3. Medical Records — Add New Medical Record, Backup Medical Records to Google Drive, and View Medical Records.
    4. Emails — Summary Emails (Optional), Email Delivery Logs, and Download Change Logs.
    5. About — full information about the app.
  5. Profile

    Your personal details and account summary. Read-only.

    What it shows: your avatar, full name, primary email (with a verification badge), recovery email (with a verification badge), phone, date of birth, age, gender, User ID (with a copy button), address, blood group, emergency contact, and general notes.

    To edit your profile or manage your account, use the avatar menu in the top-right corner, or the Settings button in the bottom navigation.

  6. Medical Records

    Store prescriptions, test reports, and other medical documents as PDFs. Every file is uploaded to your own Google Drive under /My Health Journal/Medical Records/ and a mirror copy is saved in the Administrator's Drive. The records survive across devices and sessions, and are the safest place to keep your clinical paperwork.

    Actions: add a new record (PDF only, up to 10 MB), back up the full collection to Drive, and view or open any existing record directly from the list.

  7. Avatar Menu

    The circular avatar in the top-right corner opens a quick menu with:

    • Account Management — opens Settings → Account Management.
    • Data Management — opens Settings → Backup & Exports.
    • Export PDF — generates a PDF report of your readings.
    • Medical Records — opens the Medical Records module.
    • Security — opens Security Settings directly.
    • Settings — opens the full Settings panel.
    • Logout — signs you out of the current device.

    You can also reach Settings directly from the Settings button in the bottom navigation bar.

  8. Multi-Device Sync

    You can sign in to My Health Journal on multiple devices with the same account. Every reading, profile change, and setting is synced to a secure cloud database (Firebase Firestore) in real time.

    When you add, update, or delete a reading on one device, the change appears on all other signed-in devices within seconds — without needing to refresh.

    What syncs automatically:

    • All blood pressure, pulse, and blood glucose readings
    • Your profile (name, phone, address, and other fields)
    • App settings (backup schedule, email preferences, security options)
    • Medical records metadata (adding or removing a record updates every device)
    • Email delivery logs

    What is per-device only:

    • Your Google Drive connection. Tokens are stored in the browser's local storage and are tied to the specific browser session. If you connect Drive on one device, you need to connect it separately on each additional device you sign in to. Once connected, the connection persists on that device until you manually disconnect.

    Offline use: If a device loses internet, changes are queued locally and pushed to the cloud automatically when the connection returns. Nothing is lost.
