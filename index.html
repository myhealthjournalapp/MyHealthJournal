About My Health Journal

My Health Journal is a secure, privacy-first offline application designed to help you log blood pressure and pulse readings, uncover vital trends, and maintain full control of your personal health data.

Key Features

  • AHA Color-Coded Guidance: Readings are automatically classified (Normal, Elevated, Stage 1, Stage 2, Hypertensive Crisis) following American Heart Association guidelines.
  • Measurement Score: Track consistency using a 3-readings-per-day target (Morning, Afternoon, Evening) to measure log completeness over time.
  • Contextual Tags: Optionally attach tags (medications taken, meals, post-activity) to each reading for deeper personal insights.
  • Visual Analytics: Track health trends across custom date ranges with interactive, combined, or individual line graphs for BP and Pulse.
  • Doctor-Ready Exports: Generate formatted PDF summary reports or CSV data sheets to print or share directly with your physician.
  • 100% Offline & Private: Primary health logs stay strictly on your local device. Cloud backup options rely on secure transfers to the app's designated cloud storage facility.
  • Multi-User Support: Supports up to 5 distinct user accounts on a single device.
  • App Security: Secure your account with a custom PIN or compatible Fingerprint authentication.

User Manual & Data Management

  1. Logging Readings: For maximum accuracy, log up to 3 times daily (Morning, Afternoon, Evening). Complete all three daily entries to achieve a 100% Measurement Score.
  2. Backups & Storage Options:
    • Central Cloud Facility: Sync your encrypted data directly to the App's centralized cloud storage facility. Access to this service requires account authorization and developer permission.
    • Local Storage (Recommended for Absolute Privacy): For maximum confidentiality and total control, generate local .backup files stored directly on your physical device or PC without using cloud services.
    • Restoring Data: Access Settings > Import Backup File anytime to restore from local .backup files or authorized cloud data sets.
  3. Security Updates: To change your PIN or account password, the app sends a unique One-Time Password (OTP) to your verified email address to validate the request.
